## 3. Description

The **FORMA® Pen Extender** is an extension accessory designed to be connected to Neuromuscular Electrical Stimulation (NMES) devices and administered in extraoral and intraoral areas to perform neuromuscular stimulation on motor points.

It is manufactured using non-allergenic materials, designed for easy adaptation and hygiene (washable), under the guidance and supervision of speech-language pathologists and bioengineering specialists.

---

## 4. Indications for Use / Purpose

The **FORMA® Pen Extender** is indicated to safely provide sensory stimulation, muscle contraction, analgesia, and muscle relaxation tailored to individual patient needs, following electrotherapy safety standards.

It can be utilized in Speech-Language Pathology and related fields, including:

* **Physical Therapy / Physiotherapy**;
* **Dentistry**;
* **Aesthetics**.

## 6. How to Use

Follow the steps below to operate the FORMA® Pen Extender:

1. Open the packaging and remove the FORMA® Pen Extender.
2. Turn the lock on the FORMA extender counterclockwise.
3. Connect the FORMA® Pen Extender properly to the Neuromuscular Electrical Stimulation (NMES) device.
4. Turn the lock on the FORMA extender clockwise to secure the device cable to the extender.
5. Attach the desired electrode tip to the front end of the FORMA® Pen Extender.
6. Apply conductive gel to conductive electrodes for use in extraoral regions. **Do not use gel in intraoral regions.**
7. After use, sanitize the FORMA® Pen Extender and electrodes as described in this manual and store them in their original plastic packaging. The extender and electrodes are for single-patient use only.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Usage time:** according to professional healthcare indication.

---

## 7. Washing and Sanitization Instructions

Proper hygiene of the pen pair and electrodes is mandatory, keeping them dry and clean at all times. Wash manually using neutral soap.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Sanitize before the first use and after each subsequent use.

### Sanitization Procedure

1. Wash with water and neutral liquid soap or detergent, producing abundant lather.
2. Rinse thoroughly with filtered water, removing all lather and detergent residues.
3. Dry the product with a clean, dry disposable cloth.
4. After sanitizing, spray with 70% alcohol.
5. Allow to air dry completely.
6. Wrap in clean paper towels and store inside its original packaging.

Single-person product.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not reuse this product on another individual.

## 10. Storage and Transportation

Packages containing the products must be stored and transported at room temperature, avoiding direct exposure to:

* Heat;
* Light;
* Humidity.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Transport and keep the product in a cool, dry place.

## 11. Precautions and Contraindications

### ⚠️ Precautions
* Non-sterile product.
* Do not use if packaging is open or damaged.
* Do not reuse on another individual.
* Single-patient use product.
* Transport and maintain in a cool, dry place.

### 🚫 Contraindications

The product must NOT be applied to:

* Incisions;
* Drain orifices;
* Excoriations / Abrasions;
* Open wounds.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not use if the packaging is open or damaged.
